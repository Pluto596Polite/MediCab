## Full description (max 4000)

MediCab keeps your medicine cabinet sorted. Add what's in your cupboard, and MediCab tells you what's fresh, what's about to expire and what needs replacing, before it catches you out.

SCAN IT IN SECONDS
Point your camera at a pack. MediCab reads the label right on your phone, filling in the name, strength, pack size and expiry date. It also understands barcodes, including the square codes on medicine packs that hold the expiry date. Prefer typing? Add anything by hand.

KNOW WHAT IT'S FOR
MediCab can look up common uses for a medication and add them to its notes, so you remember what each one is for. (This is general information. Always follow the pack leaflet and your pharmacist or doctor.)

NEVER MISS AN EXPIRY DATE
• A daily reminder when something is close to expiring, at a time you choose
• Choose how far ahead to be warned: 7 days to a year
• An alerts screen showing what's expired and what's coming up
• Tips on how to get rid of old medicine safely

YOUR CABINET IN 3D
Every medicine appears on a cartoon shelf as its own pack: pill boxes, bottles, syrup bottles, jars, fizz tubes, cream tubes and blister packs, each printed with its name, category and expiry date. Tap one to spin it around in 3D.

KEEP TRACK OF DOSES
Log a dose with one tap and MediCab counts down what's left in the pack. Your profile shows a day-by-day history of every dose.

ORGANISED YOUR WAY
• Categories such as pain relief, inflammation, allergy, cold & flu and vitamins, or type your own
• Note where each item is kept: bathroom, kitchen, bedroom or first aid kit
• Search and filter your whole cabinet

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no tracking. Your cabinet and dose history stay on your phone. Online lookups only send a barcode number or medication name, and you can switch them off.

MediCab is a personal organiser. It does not give medical advice, diagnose or recommend treatment.
