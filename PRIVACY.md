# MediCab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

MediCab is a medicine cabinet app. It helps you keep track of the medication you have at home, when it expires, and the doses you take. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

**In short:** your medication list, dose history and profile name stay on your phone. MediCab has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The only things that leave your phone are a barcode number or a medication name, and only when you scan or look something up with online lookups switched on.

## Information stored on your phone

MediCab saves the following in the app's private storage on your device:

- The medication you add: name, strength, form, category, pack size and stock, where it's kept, expiry date, barcode, notes and the 3D pack type.
- Your dose history: which medication you logged and when.
- Your settings, including your profile name and reminder preferences.

This information is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not run any servers that receive it. It is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall MediCab.

## Camera

MediCab uses the camera only while the scanner is open, to read barcodes and the text printed on a medicine pack. This happens on your phone using Google ML Kit. Camera images are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

## Information sent over the internet

When **Look things up online** is switched on in Settings (it is on by default), MediCab sends:

- **Barcode numbers** you scan or type, to look up the product's name and details, to: Open Products Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Food Facts (Open Food Facts association), openFDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration) and UPCitemdb.
- **A medication's name or active ingredient**, to find its common uses for the notes, to openFDA and Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation).

These requests contain only the barcode number or the name being looked up. They do not include your medication list, dose history, profile name or any account details. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy, and like any website it receives your device's IP address when it is contacted.

You can switch online lookups off at any time in **Profile › Reminders & settings › Look things up online**. Scanning and everything else then works entirely offline. The "Find common uses" button only connects when you tap it.

When **Check for updates** is switched on in Settings (it is on by default), MediCab asks GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) for the latest MediCab release each time it opens, and downloads the new version from GitHub if you choose to update. The request contains nothing about you or your cabinet; GitHub receives your device's IP address, as with any website. You can switch this off and use **Check now** instead.

Importing and exporting files happens entirely on your phone. MediCab only reads the file you pick and only writes to the place you choose.

Google ML Kit, which reads barcodes and text on your device, may send Google anonymous information about how the scanning features perform (for example, device type and processing times). It does not send your camera images or the text it reads. See [Google's ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

## Notifications

MediCab can show reminders when medication is about to expire. These are created on your phone; no reminder data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app or in Android's settings.

## Health information

Common uses shown in MediCab come from public sources and are general information, not medical advice. Always follow the pack leaflet and the advice of your pharmacist or doctor.

## Children

MediCab is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

- Edit or delete any medication, dose or your profile name in the app at any time.
- Switch off online lookups in Settings.
- Uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone.
